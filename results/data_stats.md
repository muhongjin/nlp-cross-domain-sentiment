## Duplicate removal

Duplicates are detected on normalised text (lowercase, whitespace collapsed) across all splits and all datasets. The first occurrence wins, so a review that appears in both IMDb and Amazon is kept in IMDb only. Screening covers exact normalised matches; near-duplicates with different wording are not removed and are noted as a limitation.

## Label construction

| Domain | Source | Positive class |
|---|---|---|
| imdb | stanfordnlp/imdb | rating >= 7 positive, <= 4 negative, 5-6 dropped |
| yelp | fancyzhx/yelp_polarity | 1-2 stars negative, 3-4 stars positive, 5 stars dropped |
| amazon | fancyzhx/amazon_polarity | 1-2 stars negative, 4-5 stars positive, 3 stars dropped |

The three datasets define the positive class differently. Yelp labels 3-4 stars as positive and drops 5-star reviews, so its positive class is weaker than IMDb's (7+/10) and Amazon's (4-5 stars). This is a known confound and is discussed in the report.
